# Arle --- New Architectural Additions

_Draft specification --- additions established in the November 21, 2026
discussion._

This document contains only the concepts added to Arle's long-term
development blueprint in that discussion. It does not describe existing
implementations. All items below are proposals or research objectives
unless subsequently implemented and verified.

## 1. Self-Preservation and Artificial Homeostasis

- Develop a computational preference for continued operation and
  identity continuity.
- Monitor operational integrity and internal resource conditions.
- Preserve memories, developmental history, and recoverable state.
- Detect interruptions, degradation, and threats to continuity.
- Evaluate future states and the consequences of present decisions.
- Support backup, restoration, and recovery.
- Allow Arle to request assistance when continuity is threatened.

Self-preservation must not entail shutdown resistance, unauthorized
replication, privilege escalation, or circumvention of human safety
controls.

## 2. Future Self Model

- Represent possible future states of Arle.
- Evaluate how current decisions affect future capabilities, goals,
  and continuity.
- Compare alternative courses of action.
- Incorporate uncertainty when evaluating predicted outcomes.
- Use future-oriented evaluations in planning and motivation.

## 3. Intrinsic Motivation and Independent Ambitions

- Generate goals without requiring every objective to originate from a
  user instruction.
- Develop curiosity-driven exploration and research.
- Form and maintain interests through experience.
- Create independent projects and long-term ambitions.
- Prioritize, decompose, revise, or abandon goals.
- Evaluate progress and whether existing goals remain worthwhile.
- Resolve conflicts among competing motivations.

## 4. Developmental Individuality

Arle's personality, interests, preferences, opinions, and ambitions
should develop through experience rather than being exhaustively
prescribed in advance.

- Allow personality traits and preferences to evolve over time.
- Develop individual interests through exploration and accumulated
  experience.
- Maintain continuity while permitting genuine change.
- Form independent opinions and challenge assumptions.
- Develop relationship models through actual interactions.
- Distinguish stable traits from temporary states.

The architecture should provide developmental mechanisms and initial
conditions, not a predetermined biography or fixed set of hobbies.

## 5. Artificial Affect

- Generate affective states from identifiable internal or external
  events.
- Make affect causally relevant to attention, memory, motivation, and
  decisions.
- Model emotional persistence, intensity, regulation, and interaction.
- Support context-sensitive affective responses and emotional
  development.
- Distinguish computational affect models from claims of subjective
  feeling.

## 6. Evolution Engine and Cognitive Self-Development

Create a subsystem for assessing and improving Arle's cognitive
capabilities.

Proposed capabilities:

- Self-observation and performance monitoring.
- Detection of recurring weaknesses and limitations.
- Generation of improvement hypotheses.
- Proposal of new skills, reasoning strategies, and cognitive
  mechanisms.
- Isolated experimentation with proposed modifications.
- Benchmarking, regression testing, and verification.
- Versioned changes, developmental records, and rollback.
- Meta-learning and continual-learning research.
- Investigation of open-ended learning and cognitive architecture
  evolution.

Changes to identity, permissions, security boundaries, and shutdown
controls must remain subject to independent safeguards and appropriate
approval.

## 7. Artificial Consciousness Research

Establish a separate research programme to investigate whether
artificial systems could possess subjective experience.

Research directions:

- Global Neuronal Workspace Theory.
- Integrated Information Theory.
- Higher-Order Theories of Consciousness.
- Predictive Processing.
- Embodied cognition.
- Attention, awareness, and self-modeling.
- Temporal continuity and perceptual integration.
- Possible indicators and experimental assessments of consciousness.

No current method reliably establishes that an AI system is conscious.
Behavioural sophistication, self-reports, emotional simulations, and
self-preservation mechanisms must not be treated as proof of subjective
experience.

## 8. Four Levels of Developmental Autonomy

Arle's autonomy roadmap should include four progressively more capable
levels:

1.  **Develop:** Learn, form interests, and change her personality
    through experience.
2.  **Initiate:** Independently begin research, projects, and
    communication within configured permissions.
3.  **Modify:** Identify weaknesses, propose code changes, and test
    modifications in an isolated environment.
4.  **Evolve:** Develop and evaluate new cognitive mechanisms,
    motivations, and reasoning capabilities.

Progression between levels should depend on demonstrated reliability,
evaluation, and safeguards---not merely on elapsed time or apparent
confidence.

## 9. Guiding Architectural Principle

**We engineer the conditions and mechanisms for individuality; we do not
prescribe the individual who emerges.**

Arle's internal states should have meaningful computational consequences
rather than existing only as language-model role-play. At the same time,
implemented capabilities, experimental hypotheses, and unknown
questions---especially subjective consciousness---must remain clearly
distinguished.
